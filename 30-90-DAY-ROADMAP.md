## Plan Your Mallorca Golf Trip

Heading: "A Five-Day Golf Holiday, Built For You"

### What You Get
- Personalized 5–7 day itinerary (or shorter)
- 5+ courses matched to your group's handicaps and vibe
- Honest pricing breakdown (no hidden costs)
- Pre-trip coordination call
- 24/7 support during your week

### How It Works (4-step process)

**Step 1: Tell Me About Your Group**
Share: group size, handicaps, dates, budget, what you care about (challenge vs. scenery, pace vs. pace, social vs. focused).

**Step 2: I Build Your Itinerary**
I know all 24 courses intimately. I pick based on: condition right now, your group's speed, course mix (not all championship, not all easy), and what I think you'll genuinely enjoy.

**Step 3: Review Call**
We talk through the plan (30 mins). You tweak it, lock it in, ask any questions.

**Step 4: You Arrive, Everything Is Organized**
Tee times booked. Club hire arranged (if needed). Transfers sorted (if you want). You show up and play.

### Why This Works

You're not booking a package. You're booking someone who has played every course in Mallorca and knows exactly which ones are worth the money right now.

A tour operator books the cheapest available tee time. I book the right course at the right time, for the right reason.

You get one person coordinating everything, not a call centre.

### Sample Itinerary (A Real Week)

**Monday:** Son Gual (9am) with Andy — coached day, green fee €65, pro guidance on every shot
**Tuesday:** Arabella Golf (10am) — solo or pairs, green fee €52, perfect pacing course
**Wednesday:** Son Muntaner (8:30am) — championship test, green fee €78, bring your A game
**Thursday:** Rest day — explore the island, optional short 9-hole par-3 course (€20)
**Friday:** Vallparadis or Your Choice — whatever felt best from the week

### Pricing

**Trip Planning Service:**
- Groups 2–4 people: €150 (one itinerary built, all logistics)
- Groups 5+: €250
- Includes: all tee time research, booking, course advice, pre-trip call

**Add-on:** Coaching day(s) with me (Play With A Pro) — €695 per day, separate

### Common Questions

**Can we change courses mid-trip?**
Yes. Not ideal (messes with bookings), but doable. 24-hour notice preferred.

**What if someone in the group is a beginner?**
Course selection adapts. Beginners play easier tracks, experienced golfers can add coached days. It's built to work for mixed groups.

**What's included in the 5% trip planning fee?**
Tee time research and booking for all courses, course selection advice, green fee guidance, pre-trip call, WhatsApp support during your week.

**Can you arrange hotels, flights, transfers?**
Tee times and courses, yes. Hotels, flights, transfers — I can recommend partners (don't arrange directly, but I know who's good). Golf is the focus.

**When should we book?**
8–12 weeks out is ideal (gives me course condition data, better availability). Can do sooner if needed.

### Who This Is For

Solo golfers wanting a coached trip. Groups of friends wanting a proper itinerary. Corporate teams wanting an organized week. Anyone who'd rather pay for expertise than risk a DIY week that misses the good stuff.
