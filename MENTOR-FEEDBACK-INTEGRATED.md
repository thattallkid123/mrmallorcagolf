## NEW: SEO Strategy (Search Console + Ubersuggest)

Your mentor is right — you're leaving ranking opportunities on the table. You need to know:

1. What people are searching for (Google Search Console)
2. What keywords competitors rank for (Ubersuggest)
3. What long-tail phrases you can own (low competition, high intent)

### Phase 1: Audit Current Performance (Week 1–2 | 3 Hours)

**In Google Search Console:**

1. Go to Performance report
2. Note top 10 queries you're already ranking for (even if not page 1)
3. Look for patterns:
   - "best golf courses Mallorca" — you rank, but where?
   - "golf lessons Mallorca" — ranking? Position?
   - "how much does golf cost in Mallorca" — this is a goldmine (your mentor flagged it)
   - "Mallorca golf trip planning" — should rank high

4. Document:
   - Query
   - Current impression count (how many times shown)
   - Current average position (position 4, 10, 25, etc.)
   - Current CTR (clicks / impressions)

**What you're looking for:**
- Queries where you rank positions 4–10 (quick win zone — can push to page 1 with optimization)
- Queries where you rank position 1–3 but low CTR (means your title/description isn't compelling)
- Queries you're not ranking for at all (identify gaps)

**Template to use (Google Sheet):**
```
Query | Position | Impressions | CTR | Notes
"golf courses Mallorca" | 4 | 450 | 3.2% | Add to meta desc
"Mallorca golf green fees" | 12 | 200 | 1.8% | Opportunity
"golf trip planning Mallorca" | 2 | 320 | 5.1% | Good CTR
```

### Phase 2: Ubersuggest Competitor Analysis (Week 2–3 | 4 Hours)

**Use Ubersuggest to find long-tail keywords:**

1. Go to ubersuggest.com
2. Search keyword: "golf Mallorca"
3. Look at keyword suggestions — filter by:
   - Keyword difficulty: Low (< 30)
   - Search volume: 100–500/month (sweet spot — enough search, not competitive)
   - Intent: Commercial or "how to" (not just informational)

**Examples you should find:**
- "best golf clubs to rent Mallorca" (medium volume, low competition) — **NEW OPPORTUNITY**
- "golf package deals Mallorca" (medium volume, low competition) — **NEW OPPORTUNITY**
- "short golf courses Mallorca" (low volume, very low competition) — **QUICK WIN**
- "Mallorca golf spa package" (low volume, low competition) — **BUNDLE OPPORTUNITY**

4. For each keyword with low difficulty + decent volume:
   - Click to see which sites rank
   - Check their content (how long, what structure, how old)
   - Ask: "Can I outrank this?"

5. Document your findings:
```
Keyword | Volume/Month | Difficulty | Competitors | Content Gap | Action
"golf package deals Mallorca" | 180 | 15 | 3 | No dedicated page | CREATE
"short golf courses Mallorca" | 90 | 12 | 2 | Brief mentions only | EXPAND
"golf club rental Mallorca" | 120 | 22 | 4 | One competitor page | CREATE BETTER
```

**Why this matters:**
- "golf package deals" = people ready to spend money (high intent)
- "short golf courses" = specific pain point (easy to own)
- "golf club rental" = revenue extension you haven't tapped
