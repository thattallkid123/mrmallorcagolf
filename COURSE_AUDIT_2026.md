## New guide post ideas (zero equivalents on site)

**"The Spanish Golf Licence Explained"**
Every visitor needs this. €3 at most courses, €2 at Palma Pitch & Putt. How to get an annual federation card. Covers third-party liability, what happens if you don't have one. Easy SEO win.

**"Best Practice Facilities in Mallorca"**
TopTracer at Son Muntaner (10 bays) and Alcanada. TrackMan at Pula and Son Gual Performance Academy. Son Antem's 200-bay circular range. ADN Golf indoor simulators. Good differentiation from standard course content.

**"Mallorca Golf Membership Guide"**
Specific prices make this useful: Andratx (€2,590–€6,180 depending on type), T Club (~€3,000 shared Calvià + Palma), Son Antem tiered (6/9/12-month), Alcanada unlimited annual. Contrast with invitation-only clubs (Son Gual, Bendinat).

**"History of Golf in Mallorca"**
Clear narrative arc: Son Vida 1964 → Son Servera 1967 → Santa Ponsa I 1977 → T Golf Calvià 1978 → Andratx 2000 → Son Gual + Son Quint 2007. Tournament milestones woven in (Seve 1990, DP World Tour 2022/23, Rolex Challenge Tour Grand Final).

**"Best Holes in Mallorca Golf"**
Photographic/visual guide. Son Quint 8th (Cathedral backdrop), Andratx 6th (609m, longest par-5 in Spain), Son Muntaner 15th (Sa Capitana olive tree), Canyamel 9th (stone hut in fairway), Capdepera 15th (mountain panorama), Santa Ponsa I 10th (590m, Europe's longest par-5).

**"Celebrity Golf in Mallorca"**
Tiger Woods at Son Quint (July 2022), Rafa Nadal at Capdepera (regular), Seve at Son Vida (1990 win), Federer + Nadal at Pula (July 2025 — verify), Obama, Jon Rahm, Boris Becker, Claudia Schiffer, Princess Birgitta. Strong social traffic potential.

**"Stay and Play Golf in Mallorca"**
Pula Golf Resort (Olazábal + boutique hotel), Son Antem Marriott (2 courses + spa + apartments), La Reserva Rotana (wine estate + private 9-hole), Steigenberger Andratx (hotel partner), Hotel de Mar Bendinat. What each package includes.

**"Indoor Golf in Mallorca — ADN Golf"**
Currently zero coverage on site. ADN Golf Palma: 6 Foresight bays, 500m², private rooms, leagues, year-round. Short post, fills a real gap.

**"Grass Types on Mallorca Golf Courses"**
Technical but useful for serious golfers. Alcanada: paspalum fairways (salt-tolerant) + bentgrass greens. Son Muntaner: Bermuda Celebration fairways + V8 creeping bent greens. Why drought-tolerant grasses matter for sustainability and playability. Niche but good for authority.
