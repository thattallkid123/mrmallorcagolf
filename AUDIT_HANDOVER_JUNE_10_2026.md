### Mr Mallorca Notes - course prompt pack
Use these as the first-pass questions for the 8 core guides. Keep the answers short and practical.

**Son Gual**
- What is the best tee time for scoring here?
- What wind or weather makes it play toughest?
- Where do visitors most often lose shots?
- What clubhouse tip should first-timers know?

**Alcanada**
- Which holes or views should visitors not miss?
- Where does the course bite for amateurs?
- Is there a better side of the tee box to favour anywhere?
- What is the best way to finish the round well?

**Son Muntaner**
- What makes this one feel more premium than the average Palma round?
- Which tee shots matter most?
- Where do good players still get caught out?
- What should visitors know about the clubhouse or post-round stop?

**Golf Andratx**
- Which holes are the real strategic tests?
- Where should players take the safer line?
- What mistakes matter most for mid-handicaps?
- What is the best visitor mindset for this course?

**Son Termes**
- What makes this a good or bad fit for a visitor's trip?
- Which holes punish loose swings most?
- When is the best time to play it?
- What should someone expect versus the big-name resort courses?

**T Golf Calvia**
- Which carries or blind elements matter most?
- Where do the greens ask the toughest questions?
- What should visitors avoid if they want a smooth round?
- Which handicap range suits it best?

**Santa Ponsa 1**
- Where can visitors score if they stay disciplined?
- Which holes punish greed or over-aggression?
- What does a smart course management plan look like here?
- Who is this a strong fit for?

**Son Antem West**
- What type of group or golfer suits this course best?
- Is it a good first Mallorca round?
- Where do players tend to give shots away?
- When would you choose this over the better-known names?
