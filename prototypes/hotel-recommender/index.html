Question 1 of 6

Which part of Mallorca are you planning to golf in?

This determines which course clusters you can reach easily. If you are unsure, choose Southwest: it has the most courses.

Southwest Mallorca
Andratx, Santa Ponsa, Bendinat, Calvià. The main golf cluster: 4 to 5 courses within 15 minutes. Best base for a golf-heavy trip.
North Mallorca
Alcúdia, Pollença, Formentor. Alcanada is one of the island's best courses. Fewer rounds available but a more scenic, relaxed base.
East Mallorca
Capdepera, Canyamel, Pula, Son Servera, Vall d'Or. Quieter, great beaches, underrated courses. Good for families or those who want calm over convenience.
Near Palma
Use Palma as an evening base. Son Gual, Son Vida, Son Muntaner, and Bendinat all within 20 minutes. Good if city restaurants and nightlife matter as much as golf.
Northwest: Tramuntana experience
Sóller, Deià, Valldemossa. Spectacular scenery. Not a golf base: nearest course is 50 minutes. Best as a 2-night add-on to another area.
