## Instagram Plan

Instagram collaborator posts are good for reach and trust. They are not usually strong SEO links by themselves because Instagram links are limited, often nofollow, and not as crawlable as website links.

Use Instagram as the door-opener. The SEO win comes from asking for the partner's website/newsletter/resource page link after the post.

### Existing Instagram assets

There is already a carousel generator and caption workflow in:

- `carousels/carousel_gen.py`
- `carousels/HOW_TO_MAKE_MORE_CAROUSELS.md`
- `carousels/INSTAGRAM_CAPTIONS.md`

Existing carousel/caption work already covers:

- Alcanada
- Son Muntaner
- Santa Ponsa 1

Next carousels to make from current blogs:

1. T Golf Calvia - good because the review/indexing work is fresh and the course has partner potential.
2. Son Gual - strongest premium/course-quality story.
3. Golf de Andratx - strong visuals and southwest Mallorca relevance.
4. Son Termes - distinct, local, family-friendly, more characterful.
5. Son Antem West - useful resort/hotel angle.

### Collaborator post formats

Use these formats repeatedly:

1. Course review carousel
   - Tag the course.
   - Make the hook specific: "What a PGA pro would tell a visitor before booking T Golf Calvia."
   - Link target: the full review.
   - Off-platform ask: "Could this be added to your website/news/newsletter as an independent visitor guide?"

2. Hotel/concierge carousel
   - Topic: "3 golf days that work from Palma / Bendinat / Santa Ponsa / Alcudia."
   - Tag the hotel, villa agency, or concierge partner.
   - Link target: trip planning guide or best courses guide.
   - Off-platform ask: "Could guests use this as your golf planning resource?"

3. Supplier partner post
   - Partner examples: club hire, transfers, restaurants, private chefs.
   - Topic: "What visitors forget when planning golf in Mallorca."
   - Link target: cost guide, club hire guide, or trip planning guide.
   - Off-platform ask: "Could we both link to useful planning resources rather than just tag each other?"

4. Client day proof post
   - Use real client photos only with permission.
   - Topic: "What a Play With A Pro day actually looks like."
   - Link target: Play With A Pro or A Day at Son Gual.
   - Off-platform ask: mainly referral/lead trust, less backlink focused.

### Instagram caption rule

Every collaborator caption should include one line like:

> Full first-hand guide: mrmallorcagolf.com/guides/[slug]

Even if it is not clickable in the caption, it reinforces the URL and makes the off-platform ask obvious.

## What To Avoid

- Buying backlinks.
- Random SEO directories.
- Guest post farms.
- Sitewide footer link swaps.
- Exact-match anchor text trades.
- Irrelevant "write for us" websites.
- Mass cold email with the same pitch.

The best links here should look obvious to a human: Mallorca golf, Mallorca travel, hotels, concierges, courses, and practical visitor services.
