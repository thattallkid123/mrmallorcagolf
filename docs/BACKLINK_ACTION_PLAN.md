# Mr Mallorca Golf Backlink Action Plan

Last updated: 2026-09-05 (refresh of the 2026-05-24 plan in `docs/archive/BACKLINK_ACTION_PLAN.md`)

## What changed since the May plan

The May plan never actually started — checked 2026-09-05: no outreach tracker, no media kit, no new carousels beyond the 3 that already existed before the plan was written, and **zero GA4 referral traffic since 2026-05-24 from any target on the list** (hotels, ABC Mallorca, SeeMallorca, golf clubs — nothing). It stalled at the planning stage.

Two things are different now and change the plan itself, not just its status:

1. **The plan's proposed "linkable asset to build" already exists.** May's plan called for building a "Mallorca Golf Cost Index 2026" as the next big citable asset. `/tools/green-fees` ("Compare 24 Courses - Fees & Difficulty") already does this — live, interactive, covers green fees/buggy/walking rules/par/difficulty/handicap limits for all 24 courses. Nobody has ever pitched it to anyone. The task is not "build the asset," it's "start using the asset."
2. **Three more tools exist that the May plan didn't know about**, each mapping onto an existing pitch tier:
   - `/tools/golf-day-builder` — "Eight questions. A complete day plan built around your group." Direct fit for the hotel/concierge pitch (a done-for-you guest resource).
   - `/tools/hotel-recommender` — "Six questions. A personalised hotel shortlist matched to your golf trip." Double angle for hotel outreach: cite it, *and* it recommends their property.
   - `/tools/course-selector` — the most-used tool on the site by a wide margin (58 views/90d vs. 8-17 for the others). Best general-purpose "which course should I play" pitch asset.
3. **Carousels are dropped from this plan.** The MMG Instagram account is now running under its own plan (see `/social-media-mmg`). Per the May plan's own logic, Instagram links were only ever a door-opener toward a real website/newsletter link, never the SEO win itself — so IG production doesn't need to live inside the backlink plan at all anymore.

Everything else in the May plan — the 5 target tiers, the outreach tracker, the media kit, the "what to avoid" list — held up under review and carries forward unchanged.

## Backlink Target List (unchanged structure, tool-first pitches)

### Tier 1: Editorial and local media

Targets: ABC Mallorca, SeeMallorca, Mallorca Magazine, Majorca Daily Bulletin, local lifestyle magazines, luxury travel blogs covering Mallorca.

**Lead with the tool, not a guide:**

> "I'm a PGA professional based in Mallorca. I've built a live comparison tool covering all 24 of the island's golf courses - green fees, buggy costs, difficulty, handicap limits - plus first-hand 2026 course reviews. Happy to provide an expert quote, or the tool itself may be worth linking as a resource for readers planning a golf trip."

Ask for: expert quote with link, guest article, source citation, "further reading"/tool link from an existing Mallorca golf/travel article.

### Tier 2: Golf clubs and course partners

Targets: courses already reviewed, courses with strong photos/first-hand notes, visitor-friendly courses wanting international golfers.

Pitch (unchanged from May): independent PGA review, offered for them to share if useful. Ask for a news/social share, a link from a "press"/"visitors"/"reviews" page, or a newsletter mention. Start with the courses where the review is positive and fair — don't ask every course immediately.

### Tier 3: Hotels, villas, and concierges

Targets: Palma hotels, Bendinat/Illetas/Portals, Santa Ponsa/Andratx, Alcudia/Pollensa (for Alcanada), luxury villa agencies, concierge services.

**Lead with Golf Day Builder or Hotel Recommender, not the trip-planning guide:**

> "Your guests often ask where to play golf in Mallorca. I've built two free tools they can use directly - one plans a full golf day around their group in 8 questions, the other matches them to the right hotel area for their golf trip. Happy to also help plan rounds or accompany guests on course."

Ask for: link from guest resources, blog collaboration, concierge supplier listing, newsletter feature.

### Tier 4: Practical travel partners

Targets: club hire companies, airport transfer companies, private drivers, bike/car rental serving golf tourists, restaurants near courses, private chefs.

Lead asset: Course Selector tool (most broadly useful, highest existing traffic) or the cost/club-hire guides. Pitch and asks unchanged from May.

### Tier 5: Golf communities and forums

Unchanged from May. Be useful first, mention the relevant guide/tool only when it genuinely helps, keep tone human and transparent.
