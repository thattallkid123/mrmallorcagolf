# `/reel` — Instagram Reel & Shorts Generator

**Use when:** You have 10–35 seconds of footage (swing, course, client reaction) and want a ready-to-post caption, cover text, and Douyin brief.

Input: course name (if course-focused) + angle (one hole decision, client reaction, three greens, swing fix, cost breakdown, first tee, Mandarin insight).

Output: hook, shot list, caption, hashtags, cover-frame text, Douyin brief.

---

## Step 1: Gather footage and choose the angle

**Footage types you have:**
- Swings of you (Andrew)
- Client swings + reactions  
- Course clips (brief flyovers, fairways, greens, specific holes)
- Lesson footage (fault + drill + result)

**Angles (pick one):**

| Angle | Best for | Hook formula | What to film |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One hole decision** | Flagship courses (Son Gual, Alcanada, T Golf Calvià) | "[Hole] at [course] has [ruined/won] more [thing]. The play is [specific strategy]." | Tee shot or approach, 10–15 sec. Show the lie, the difficulty. |
| **Client reaction** | Play-with-a-pro days, genuine moments | "[Achievement] on [course]." | One good shot + their genuine reaction, 10–20 sec. No setup. |
| **Three greens** | Value courses, visual variation | "[Course]: [name the greens by hole number]." | Three 5-second clips of different greens, text labels, no talking. |
| **Swing fix** | Your coaching content | "[Fault name] — the fix." | Fault clip + drill + result, 20–25 sec. Text overlay names the fault in plain words. |
| **Cost breakdown** | Pricing content (Peak/Low green fees) | "What €[price] gets you at [course]." | Course beauty shots + text overlays showing conditions, pace, practice areas, honest negative. |
| **First tee POV** | Opening-hole content | "This is [hole] at [course]. [One specific thing about it]." | Tee shot or setup clip, 10–15 sec. Your voiceover or text. |
| **Mandarin insight** | Chinese audience, your differentiator | "[Coaching insight or course fact] in Mandarin" | You speaking in Mandarin or English with Mandarin subtitles. 20–30 sec. |

---

## Step 2: Draft the hook and caption

Use the voice guidelines. Read `MMG_BRAND_VOICE_GUIDELINES.md` (Drive: Systems & Planning). Key rules:

- **Hook:** specific thing + course name + surprising truth. No setup, no questions.
- **Caption:** 150–200 words (or 80–120 if quick). Story-led, not broad. Opens on a moment.
- **Hard bans:** em dashes, "stunning", "nestled", "the best part?", "here's the truth", "it's not X it's Y", "whether you're...", three-word fragments.
- **One honest negative:** every post needs it.
- **One CTA:** save, comment, share, link, or follow. Never two.

**Hook examples:**
- "The 16th at Son Gual has destroyed more cards than any hole on the island. The play is 3-wood short of the water, not driver."
- "First time breaking 90 abroad — this is what a good round looks like on Mallorca's best course."
- "€165 gets you immaculate fairways, demanding greens, and one thing that catches everyone: the par 3s."

**Caption structure:**
1. Hook line (the hook above).
2. The observation (what it means, who it suits, what most people get wrong).
3. The honest bit (one negative, one specific detail).
4. Close (one CTA or genuine question).

---

## Step 3: Prepare the cover frame and overlay text

The Instagram app's built-in text tool is what you'll use. Keep these simple:

- **Cover frame:** Screenshot the best single frame from your footage. Test it — if it reads well on a phone, it's good.
- **Overlay text:** One line, max. Place it in a corner so it doesn't obscure the footage. Examples:
  - "The 16th — 185m into the wind"
  - "First time breaking 90 abroad"
  - "€165 at Son Gual: Peak rate"
  - "The fix: early extension"

**Text styling:** Use Instagram's default white text with a subtle shadow or outline so it reads over any background.

---

## Step 4: Generate 3 hashtags

Apply this rule exactly:
1. **Course-specific:** `#CourseName` (e.g., `#SonGual`). If the course name is two words, run them together or hyphenate.
2. **Destination:** `#MallorcaGolf` (always this one, ~50k posts, findable and not drowned). 
3. **Intent:** Pick one based on the angle:
   - For course/strategy content: `#GolfTravel`
   - For client stories: `#GolfHoliday`
   - For coaching/tips: `#GolfCoaching`
   - For value/pricing: `#GolfMallorca`

---

## Step 5: Film, trim, and post

**Trimming in-app (Instagram native, no external edit needed):**
1. Open Instagram app.
2. Create new post → select Reel.
3. Upload the clip you've shot.
4. Use Instagram's trimmer to cut it down to 10–35 seconds.
5. Add the overlay text (one line, corner placement).
6. Add a cover frame (Instagram will show a frame selector).

**Posting:**
1. Paste the caption (from this brief).
2. Add the 3 hashtags.
3. Tag the course (geotag + mention, e.g., `@golfsonantem`).
4. If it's a client reel, invite the client or their partner as a **Collab** (not just a tag) — posts appear on their grid, too.
5. Schedule for Monday (course content), Wednesday (image post, not a reel), or Friday (client stories).

## Step 6: Generate the Douyin brief

If the reel is worth Douyin/RedNote, generate a parallel brief:

**Douyin adaptations:**
- Hook stays the same in English or Mandarin (your choice; Mandarin reaches broader audience).
- Caption becomes 2–3 sentences max (Douyin users scroll faster).
- Add a Douyin-specific CTA: "Save for your Mallorca golf trip" or "Follow for weekly course insights."
- If it's a Mandarin reel, use simplified Chinese, keep the PGA credential visible.

Hand this brief to the `/chinese-content` skill if you want a full Douyin post.

## Step 7: Voice self-check (before posting)

Run these checks on the caption:

1. **Em dashes?** Search the caption. Remove every one. Replace with comma, colon, or full stop.
2. **Banned words?** Search: stunning, nestled, seamless, elevate, breathtaking, hidden gem, vibrant, exceptional, world-class (as filler), genuinely (as filler), unparalleled, boasting, dynamic, cutting-edge.
3. **Three-fragment sequences?** Delete them: ~~"The views. The course. The experience."~~
4. **Honest negative?** Is there one? (Bunker inconsistency, signage, wind exposure, pace, etc.)
5. **One CTA only?** Save, comment, share, link, follow — not two.
6. **Opening sentence on any golf site?** Could it? If yes, rewrite to be specific to Andy's experience or this course.
7. **AI patterns?** No "The best part?", "Here's the truth", "What people don't realise...", "It's not X, it's Y", "Whether you're...", "In the heart of...", "If you're looking for...".
8. **British English?** Check: colour, organised, favour.
9. **Place names?** "Mallorca" not "Majorca". Accents on Catalan names (e.g., "Calvià").
10. **Credentials earned?** Did Andy actually play this course? (Hermes `played-vs-researched` rule: firsthand-played pool is Son Gual, Alcanada, Santa Ponsa 1 & 3, Andratx, Son Muntaner, T Golf Calvià, Son Antem West, Son Termes). If researched only, label it.

## Step 8: Cross-post to YouTube Shorts and TikTok (optional, once habit is established)

Once you have 2–3 reels, export the same vertical video file and upload:

- **YouTube Shorts:** same caption, same hashtags, same cover frame.
- **TikTok:** same, but adapt CTA slightly ("Link in bio to book a round").

This is free distribution to a different audience. Same file, one extra upload per reel.

---

## Tips

- **Film on phone in vertical (9:16).** Instagram and Shorts accept this natively. No aspect ratio conversion needed.
- **One reel per week minimum** to move the needle on discovery. Wednesday image posts keep the feed healthy; Monday and Friday reels are what drives reach.
- **Save every reel file** in `~/Documents/Instagram_Reels/` or a Drive folder so you can repost later or cross-post to Shorts.
- **Authenticity > production quality.** A 10-second phone clip of a client's genuine reaction beats a polished, silent flyover. Your current reels already prove this.
- **Geotag every reel** to the specific course (Instagram course pages get browsed by golfers already on the island).
- **Collab with courses and clients** — Instagram's native collab feature distributes reels to their followers at no cost.

## Example workflow (Monday reel)

**Footage:** One good tee shot at Son Gual, showing the bunker left and the fairway right.

**Angle:** One hole decision.

**Hook:** "The 16th at Son Gual has destroyed more cards than any hole on the island. The play is 3-wood short of the water, not driver."

**Caption:**
> The 16th at Son Gual has destroyed more cards than any hole on the island. The play is 3-wood short of the water, not driver.
> 
> I teed off with a 3-wood yesterday and found the fairway 140 yards in. The bunker left is the real hazard — it's deeper than it looks from the tee. Most players see the water and go driver, trying to carry it all. The fairway bunker catches you short.
> 
> The greens here are excellent but firm, so landing short is actually OK — you've got 100 yards to work with and the slope plays kindly. Miss left into that bunker and you're chipping out sideways.
> 
> Son Gual is worth a round on every Mallorca trip. If you're playing it for the first time, ask the starter which tee marker suits your game.

**Hashtags:** `#SonGual` `#MallorcaGolf` `#GolfTravel`

**Cover text:** "The 16th — driver is the mistake"

**Overlay position:** Bottom right corner, white text with shadow.

**CTA:** Save ("save this for your round")

**Post:** Monday, 10 AM local time. Tag `@golfsonantem`. Invite the course as a collab.
