## Core thesis

Own the on-island premium golf layer for wealthy visitors before hotels, villas, yachts and member clubs build it themselves.

PWAP (Play With A Pro) is the wedge, not the whole business. The larger opportunity is to become the trusted golf layer for every wealthy-guest channel landing on Mallorca.

**Positioning sentence:** Mr Mallorca Golf makes premium golf in Mallorca simple, hosted and memorable for visitors who want more than a tee time.

## Why the opportunity exists now

Mallorca has luxury hotels, villa agencies, yacht concierges, branded residences, private-jet traffic and serious golf demand. What it does not yet have is a clear on-island premium golf operator who combines local course access, PGA credibility, guest hosting, logistics and content distribution into one recognisable product.

Every wealthy-guest channel says "we can arrange golf" — almost none own a polished, transfer-included, pro-hosted product. That gap is the wedge.

## Revenue streams — the full picture

These are ranked by how quickly each can generate income with autonomo now active.

### Tier A — Income available now

**1. Play With A Pro (PWAP)**  
The core product. Hosted rounds, private coaching rounds, concierge-led tee-time organisation. Direct fee, high margin, no middleman. Requires: a price menu and a booking page. This is the proof-of-concept that makes everything else credible.

**2. Tour operator margin on trip planning**  
Andy plans and packages multi-day golf trips (hotel, villa, tee times, transfers, dining). Charge a management fee or build margin into every component — accommodation, transport, green fees, restaurants, spa. Standard tour operator model. Requires: supplier relationships + a packaged product guests can book.

**3. Affiliate and referral commissions**  
Revenue-sharing with golf courses, hotels, restaurants, transport providers, yacht charters, spas. As the website grows, curated partner links earn commissions on conversions. Low setup cost. Start with the courses already visited and the hotels already known.

### Tier B — Partner-dependent, 30–60 day horizon

**4. White-label packages for hotels and villas**  
Turnkey golf-day packages (tee time, transfers, hosted round, photography, optional spa/restaurant) that hotels and villa companies offer under their own brand. Charge a licensing/arrangement fee plus per-booking commission. E&V, Le Collectionist, Charles Marlow are the targets.

**5. Yacht shore-day packages**  
High-value shore day for owners and principals while the yacht is alongside. Bespoke "STP Shore Days" product for IPM Group (Marina Port de Mallorca + STP Shipyard). Premium pricing because the client is already spending €250k+/week on the yacht.

**6. Corporate golf days and incentive events**  
Corporate subscriptions or one-off branded days — bank of hosted rounds, priority booking, tournaments, client entertainment. Target industries with strong Mallorca presence: finance, yachting, real estate, luxury property.

### Tier C — Content and digital, build now, revenue in 90 days

**7. Digital membership / subscription**  
Members-only area on mrmallorcagolf.com offering detailed course guides, video lessons, local insider tips, partner hotel discounts, early booking privileges. Tiered (Silver/Gold/Platinum). Recurring revenue. Beta launch in 60 days.

**8. Digital course guides and travel planners**  
Downloadable PDF or interactive guides — course strategy, drone flyovers, logistics, post-round dining. Sell as standalone products or use as lead magnets to drive subscriptions and email capture.

**9. Media and content production**  
Produce high-quality content from on-course sessions — course vlogs, travel documentaries, equipment reviews. Monetise via YouTube, brand sponsorships, product placements. Offer bespoke content packages to hotels, courses, and tourism boards. The Douyin audience (hundreds of millions of views) is itself a marketable asset.

### Tier D — Longer-horizon, build the machine first

**10. Licensing outside Mallorca**  
Document the PWAP methodology and package it as a franchise/licensing opportunity for other destinations (Ibiza, Costa del Sol, Algarve). Provide training, marketing collateral, booking SOPs, CRM setup, revenue-share templates. Only credible once Mallorca has repeatable SOPs and partner proof.

**11. Events and sponsorship**  
Annual "Mallorca Golf Week" or UHNWI Invitational — sponsors pay to associate. Once proven, license the concept to other destinations.

**12. Concierge training (B2B)**  
Training programme for hotel concierges and villa hosts on golf bookings and guest experience. Sell as a service or license to hospitality schools.

**Funnel:** Douyin (awareness) → Xiaohongshu (consideration) → WeChat (conversion). HNW Chinese transact in WeChat, not Douyin.
