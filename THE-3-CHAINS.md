# THE 3 CHAINS (What Actually Moves The Needle)

**June 10 → Sept 30 | Everything Feeds Into Everything Else**

---

## Why 3 Chains?

Because growth doesn't happen in a vacuum. Testimonials make the Plan Your Trip page more credible. Plan Your Trip page gets better with course reviews. Course reviews drive email signups. Email signups let you nurture leads over time. All three together create momentum.

You can't just do one. You have to do all three, in sequence, so they compound.

---

## CHAIN 1: SOCIAL PROOF (Testimonials → Trustpilot → Booking Confidence)

```
THIS WEEK
├─ WhatsApp 5 past clients
│  ("What was the one moment that made the day worth it?")
│
├─ Set up Trustpilot account
│  (Register business, add logo, set up auto-invite)
│
└─ Collect replies + write 2–3 testimonial blocks
   (Name + photo + specific outcome)
   
WEEK 2–3
├─ Integrate 3 new testimonials across site
│  (Homepage, Play With A Pro, Plan Your Trip)
│
└─ Send Trustpilot invites (10+ first batch)
   (Every booking, auto-send invite)

ONGOING
└─ Each booking → add 1 new testimonial
   (Monthly Trustpilot request)

BY SEPT 30, YOU'LL HAVE:
├─ 8+ specific, attributed testimonials on site
├─ Trustpilot profile with 15+ reviews
└─ RESULT: Conversion rate ↑ 20–35%
           Trust level sufficient for hotel pitches
           Signature Day feels justified to prospects
```

**Why This Works:**
- A prospect sees 8 real people say "this was worth the €695–€3,000"
- They see third-party (Trustpilot) credibility backing it
- They book with confidence, not hesitation

## The Decision

**Before you start, pick your pace:**

- **Steady (5–10 hrs/week):** Focus on quality over speed. Get the core three chains working. Hotel outreach in Sept. Video in Q4.

- **Aggressive (15–20 hrs/week):** Go all-in on content moat. Hit 18 reviews by Aug. Start hotel partnerships in Aug. Credibility highest.

**Both paths work. Pick based on your actual bandwidth. Consistency matters more than speed.**

---

## What NOT To Do (Until Sept)

Skip these until you've got testimonials + Plan Your Trip + 15+ reviews:

- **Xiaohongshu account** — Valid, but requires ongoing posting. Defer.
- **Video production** — High leverage, but 6+ hours or €2k+. Defer to Q4.
- **PR campaign** — Medium priority. Better in Q4 with more reviews + Trustpilot proof.
- **Corporate golf landing page** — Overlaps with Plan Your Trip. Defer.
- **Gift vouchers** — Nice to have, not essential. 1-hour thing in August if inspired.

Do the three chains first. Everything else is bonus.
