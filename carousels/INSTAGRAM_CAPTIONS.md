## ALCANADA

**Caption:**
The most scenic course in Mallorca. By some distance.

Robert Trent Jones Jr. designed it. The lighthouse sits on its own island and is visible from 16 of the 18 holes. The greens are fast, severely undulating, and 58 bunkers force accurate approaches on almost every hole.

It hosts the Rolex Challenge Tour Grand Final — returning for its sixth time in October 2026. You play the same holes that decide a professional's card for the season.

It's also the course I take clients to when I want them to go home talking about one round in particular.

## SON MUNTANER

**Caption:**
Best Golf Course in Spain at the 2025 World Golf Awards. Five minutes from central Palma.

I played it on a full tee sheet on a Saturday morning. Here's the honest version.

The course doesn't show you everything from the tee. It reveals itself through approach play — tight landing areas, small greens, precise positioning required at every hole. Once you understand that, it feels fair. Strong shots get clear reward.

The greens were rolling pure at a solid pace and held up across a busy Saturday without losing quality. That's where the Best in Spain title starts to make sense.

Hole 15 has an ancient olive tree that has been standing for roughly 1,000 years. The Balearic government declared it a natural monument. Someone built a golf hole around it, and I'm glad they did.

## SANTA PONSA 1

**Caption:**
Ballesteros, Olazábal, Woosnam, Langer — they all played this course.

Santa Ponsa 1 hosted six European Tour events. In 2021 it brought top-level professional golf back to Mallorca after a decade away.

It's also publicly bookable, open to all visitors, and one of the easiest places on the island to enjoy hitting driver. Wide fairways, generous landing areas, and a course that builds confidence rather than grinding it down.

The 10th is one of the longest par 5s in Europe at 590m. Into the wind it plays every metre of it.

Holes 5, 6, and 7 have the best Tramuntana mountain views on this side of the island.
