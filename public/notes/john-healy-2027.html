The practical read: this east-coast group, Vall d'Or, Son Servera, Capdepera, Pula and Canyamel, sits inside about 15 to 20 minutes of Manacor, so it's the natural home cluster if you're based near the Academy. Several of them reciprocate with each other and with Andratx, Maioris and Son Termes at a flat €49 green fee, so a membership out east still buys cheap access back toward Palma. Golf Son Gual is the one course here I don't have membership pricing for yet, as it's run by a family office.

Coaching, while you're here

Whichever base you land on, we can talk through what else to add and how to make the time as useful as possible. Most clubs would likely set up a seasonal membership even where it isn't advertised. Some already run summer memberships outright, so the option is usually there even when a website doesn't show it.

For the physical side you flagged from the TPI material: John Brazier (The Golf Doctor) is the physio I'd point you to, or The Movement Mallorca in Portals, both well regarded for golf-specific work. For TPI-based training, Moritz Beyer is one I've heard good things about, and the Rafa Nadal Academy itself likely has good options too, worth asking once you're there. All English-speaking as far as I know.

Happy to build in more practice time alongside the rounds too: technical work, course strategy, whatever's best for the program for you. On the "where to base" question specifically: if the Manacor side wins out, Michel at Capdepera is great, and there are a few more coaches around that side of the island. Or if Palma / the southwest is the choice, I can help out with most of it, or with any other recommendations.

Plenty of time to get this all sorted over these days and beyond, and build the full plan together, staying flexible as your own thinking on it develops.
