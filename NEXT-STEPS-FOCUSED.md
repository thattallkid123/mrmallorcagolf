#### 2. PLAN YOUR TRIP PAGE (Week of June 15 — 2–3 Hours)
**Current state:** Thin, generic, 4/10 score  
**Goal:** 1,000–1,200 word rewrite that explains value and process  
**Why it matters:** High-commercial-intent visitors land here and bounce because it doesn't explain what they'll get.

**Structure (copy this):**
```
H1: "Plan Your Mallorca Golf Trip — How It Works"

What You Get (3–4 bullet points)
- Custom 5-course itinerary matched to your group
- Honest green fee & logistics guidance
- Pre-trip call to lock in preferences
- 24/7 coordination during the week

The Process (4 steps, one paragraph each)
- Step 1: Share your group (size, handicaps, dates, budget)
- Step 2: Andy builds your itinerary (considers pace, course mix, condition, your vibe)
- Step 3: Review call (tweak, lock in, answer questions)
- Step 4: You arrive, everything is organized

Why This Works (2–3 specific points)
- Not a generic package — it's built for YOU
- Andy knows the 24 courses from playing them, not reading reviews
- You're not coordinating with a tour company — you're coordinating with the person who'll be there

Sample Itinerary (anonymised example)
- Monday: Son Gual with Andy (coached day)
- Tuesday: Arabella (solo)
- Wednesday: Son Muntaner (group day)
- Thursday: Rest, lunch, optionals (short par-3 course)
- Friday: Your choice from the 24

FAQ (4–5 specific questions, not generic)
- Can we change courses mid-trip? (Yes, and here's how)
- What if someone in the group is a beginner? (Coaching available, course selection adapts)
- What does 5% cover? (Be specific: tee time booking, course advice, maybe a WhatsApp coordination call)
- Can we add a spa day / dinner reservation? (Yes, Andy has partner contacts)

Testimonial (1 trip-planning client)
- Include specific detail (what course combo did they choose, what did they say was the value)

CTA (2 options)
- "Tell Andy What You Need" (button)
- "Chat on WhatsApp" (button)

Pricing (brief clarity)
- "Itinerary planning is [€X / €Y for groups of X]. Add coaching days separately."
```

**Why this structure:** Answers every unasked question that makes a prospect hesitate.

**Time to write:** 2–3 hours in one sitting.

### The 20/20 Work (Adds 10% More Growth But Takes 80% of Your Time)

If you have time beyond the above, prioritize in this order:

#### 4. COURSE REVIEWS — Top 3 (July, 3–4 Hours Per Review)
**Current state:** ~9 of 24 published  
**Goal:** 24 published by year-end (the content moat)  
**Realistic pace:** 2–3 per month = 12–18 new by Dec  
**Which ones to start:** Son Muntaner, Real Club Bendinat, Arabella (highest search volume + prestige)

**Standard format (reuse this):**
```
H1: "[Course Name] Golf Course Review — [X] Words"

Headline summary: 1 sentence positioning (e.g., "Son Muntaner is Mallorca's toughest championship course — perfect for low-handicap golfers who want to be challenged")

Layout & Design: 2–3 paragraphs
- Number of holes, par, length from pro tees
- Course condition (drainage, grass quality, rough depth)
- Pace of play (how long does 18 holes take)

Difficulty & Who It's For: 2–3 paragraphs
- Green handicap range (e.g., "plays like a 1–4 handicap course")
- Specific holes that define the experience
- Best for: Solo golfers / groups / beginners / pros / couples

Green Fees & Timing: 1 paragraph
- Peak season price, low season price
- Best months to play
- Booking (1 week advance, online, etc.)

Conditions & Notes: 1–2 paragraphs
- Current drainage (wet vs. playable right now)
- Caddie quality / club hire quality
- Food & drink options

Photos: 3–5 course shots (you already have these)

Contextual CTA: "Planning to play [course name]? I run coached rounds here monthly. See how a pro day works."

Final rating: Brief 1–2 sentence summary and a 5-star rating (that becomes schema)
```

**Why this matters:** Owns "[course name] review" queries + drives organic traffic that funnels into "Play With A Pro" or "Plan Your Trip"

**Time per review:** 1.5–2 hours in a batch session.

## The Things to SKIP (For Now)

These are in the audit but not ROI-aligned for your bandwidth:

- **Xiaohongshu launch** — Valid for Chinese market, but requires sustained 2–3 posts/month. Skip until you have that cadence.
- **Video production** — High leverage, but €2,000+ or 6+ hours DIY. Defer to Q4 (Sept+) when content moat is complete.
- **PR campaign** — Medium ROI. Defer to Q4 when you have more authority (more course reviews, Trustpilot reviews, media-ready angles).
- **Corporate golf page** — Valid revenue extension, but overlaps with Plan Your Trip + Play With A Pro. Do after those are solid.
- **Hotel concierge programme** — Real upside (€20k+/year potential), but requires relationship-building energy. Put on Q4 list.
- **Gift vouchers** — Nice-to-have revenue, not critical. Can be a 1-hour thing in August if you want.

These aren't wrong — they're just not the leverage points right now.
