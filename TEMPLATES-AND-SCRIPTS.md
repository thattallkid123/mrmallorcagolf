## Plan Your Mallorca Golf Trip

### A Five-Day Golf Holiday, Built For You

You've got a week in Mallorca. You want to play the best courses, not waste 
a day at a tourist trap. You want someone who knows every green, knows what's 
playable right now, and knows how to make five days flow.

That's what I do.

### What You Get

- **Personalized itinerary** — 5–7 courses picked for your group specifically
- **Honest green fees** — no inflated pricing, no surprise costs
- **Course expertise** — I've played all 24 Mallorca courses and know their conditions right now
- **All logistics sorted** — tee times booked, transfers arranged, everything confirmed before you arrive
- **Pre-trip call** — we lock in the plan and answer any questions
- **24/7 support** — during your week, any adjustments needed

### How It Works (4 Steps)

**Step 1: Tell Me About Your Group**
- How many golfers?
- Handicaps? (Scratch to 18, or a mix?)
- Dates you're visiting?
- Budget per person?
- What matters to you? (Challenge vs. scenic, pace vs. pace, social vs. focused)

**Step 2: I Build Your Itinerary**
I know the 24 courses intimately. I pick based on:
- Current condition (green speeds, drainage, playability right now)
- Your group's pace (mixing fast courses with slower ones)
- Course variety (not all championship, not all easy — a real mix)
- What I think you'll actually enjoy (not just what sounds impressive)

**Step 3: Review Call**
We talk through the plan (30 mins). You ask questions, tweak it if needed, 
lock it in.

**Step 4: You Arrive, Everything Is Organized**
- Tee times confirmed
- Club hire arranged (if you need it)
- Transfers sorted (if you want them)
- You show up and play.

### Why This Works

**You're not booking a package.** You're booking someone who has played 
every course and knows exactly which ones are worth the money right now.

A tour operator books the cheapest available tee time. I book the right course 
at the right time, for the right reason.

You get one person coordinating everything, not a call centre rotating you 
between departments.

And because I coach on some of your days, if you want that, I know your group 
and your swing. You're not starting from zero with a stranger.

### Common Questions

**Can we change courses mid-trip if we don't like the plan?**
Not ideal (messes with tee time bookings), but possible with 24-hour notice. 
We build it right the first time so this rarely happens.

**What if someone in the group is a beginner or high handicap?**
Course selection adapts. Beginners play more forgiving tracks, and lower-handicap 
golfers can either join them for a fun day or add a separate coached day 
at a tougher course. Groups of mixed ability work great here.

**What's included in the trip planning fee?**
Research all 24 courses and pick the right 5–7 for your group. Book all tee 
times. Advise on green fees and booking windows. Make sure everything flows 
(no stupid commute times between courses). Pre-trip call to lock it in. 
WhatsApp support during your week.

**Can you arrange hotels, flights, transfers?**
Tee times and courses — yes, that's what I do. Hotels, flights, transfers — 
I can recommend my favourite partners (don't arrange them directly, but I 
know who's good). I focus on golf.

**When should we book?**
8–12 weeks out is ideal. Gives me the best course condition data and tee 
time availability. Can do it closer to the date if needed, but earlier is better.

**What if the weather is bad?**
Mallorca golf is playable almost year-round. Rain comes and goes. We plan 
around seasons, and if weather is genuinely unplayable on a day, we adjust 
tee times or courses. Rarely an issue.

**Do you offer group discounts for 6+ golfers?**
Yes. Get in touch for pricing.

## 3. COURSE REVIEW TEMPLATE

### Standard Format (Copy This)

```markdown
---
title: "[Course Name] Golf Review | Mallorca | Mr Mallorca Golf"
description: "[1-sentence hook about the course]"
---

## [Course Name] Review

**Headline Summary**
[1 sentence positioning, e.g., "Son Muntaner is Mallorca's toughest championship 
course — it demands respect from any golfer and rewards accuracy."]

### The Basics

- **Holes:** 18
- **Par:** [X]
- **Length:** [X] metres (pro tees), [X] metres (visitor tees)
- **Designer:** [If known]
- **Course Type:** [Championship / parkland / links-style / etc.]

### Layout & Playing Experience

[2–3 paragraphs describing how the course flows, key holes, what makes it distinctive]

Example:
"The course opens with two relatively straightforward holes, then tightens 
immediately at hole 3. The front nine climbs steadily inland, with water 
coming into play on holes 5, 7, and 9. The back nine is moorland-style, 
exposed to wind, with tight fairways and deep bunkers. Holes 16–18 bring 
you back to lower ground with dramatic finishing holes."

### Difficulty & Who It's For

[2–3 paragraphs on handicap range and player type]

Example:
"This is a professional-length course. Scratch to 4-handicap golfers will 
find it challenging but fair. 5–12 handicap will find it difficult but 
playable if you're strategic (lay up, avoid long carries, trust your irons). 
13+ handicap will struggle — the par-3s are long and the course punishes 
poor tee shots. Good for: golfers wanting a real test, match play groups 
(course rewards shot-making), anyone who loves Scottish-style golf."

### Green Fees & Timing

- **Peak Season (May–Oct):** €[X]
- **Low Season (Nov–Apr):** €[X]
- **Booking:** Online at [website] or phone [number]
- **Tee times:** Available 7 days in advance
- **Best months to play:** [e.g., Sept–Oct, March–April]
- **Busiest months:** July–Aug (avoid if you want pace of play)

### Course Conditions (Right Now)

[1–2 paragraphs on current playability]

Example:
"June update: Greens are running at 12–13 on the stimp (moderately fast). 
Fairways are firm and true. Rough is thick (recent rain). Bunkers are 
well-maintained, not overly punishing. The course has good drainage so 
it's playable even after rain. Plan for firm landing areas off the tee."

### Caddie & Services

- **Caddie available:** Yes (€[X]) / No
- **Caddie quality:** [Brief note on knowledge level]
- **Club hire:** Available, €[X] for full set (quality: [good/adequate/basic])
- **Food & drink:** [What's available: restaurant, snack bar, halfway house?]
- **Facilities:** [Pro shop, practice range, short game area, etc.]

### The Verdict

[2–3 sentences: final recommendation, who should play it, when]

Example:
"Son Muntaner is a genuine championship test — worth playing once to feel 
the challenge, worth playing again if you shoot a good score. It's at its 
best in spring and autumn. Bring your best game and enjoy the views from 
the high points on the course."

### Play This Course?

If you're planning to play [Course Name], I run coached rounds here regularly. 
See what a day with a pro looks like, and I'll help you get the most from 
your round.

[Link: "Play With A Pro"]
```

### What to Customize

- **Course name, par, length** — verify against official scorecard
- **Designer** — optional but adds authority
- **Layout & playing experience** — this is where your personal knowledge shines (you've played it)
- **Difficulty range** — based on your experience coaching there
- **Fees** — update quarterly (or when you know they've changed)
- **Conditions** — update seasonally (June conditions different from Dec)
- **Services** — check with the course if needed

## 6. REDDIT ANSWER FRAMEWORK

### How to Answer (Not Sell)

Don't post promotional content. Answer genuine questions and let your credibility speak.

### Example 1: "Best golf courses in Mallorca?"

❌ **Don't do this:**
"Check out my website — I review all 24 Mallorca courses and offer coaching!"

✅ **Do this instead:**
"Son Gual and Son Muntaner are the stand-outs — championship-level courses 
in great condition year-round. Arabella is underrated for pace and beauty. 
If you're looking for less-known gems, Vall d'Or on the east coast is quiet 
and stunning.

All 24 courses have something to offer depending on your handicap. I've played 
them all and reviewed each one if you want specifics.

What's your handicap and what kind of course appeals to you?"

Your bio: "PGA Coach, Mallorca. I review all 24 courses here."

### Example 2: "Planning a golf trip to Mallorca"

❌ **Don't do this:**
"I can plan your trip! DM me for details."

✅ **Do this instead:**
"Mallorca is amazing for a golf trip. A few tips:

1. Book 8–12 weeks ahead for the best tee times and weather windows
2. Mix one or two championship courses (Son Gual, Son Muntaner) with easier 
   tracks so you're not exhausted
3. Play Mon/Wed/Fri, rest Tue/Thu — this pacing works better than back-to-back
4. Green fees are €50–80 depending on season and course
5. Spring and autumn are best (May, Sept, Oct)

If you want specific course recommendations based on your handicaps, I'm 
happy to help. I know all 24 courses here."

### Cadence

- Aim for 1–2 answers per week
- Answer questions where you have genuine expertise
- Only link to your site if someone specifically asks for it
- Let your credibility build over months (this is long-term presence)
