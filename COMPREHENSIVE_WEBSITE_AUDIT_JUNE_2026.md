# 1. BUSINESS POSITIONING AUDIT

## What You're Actually Selling (and What You're Not)

**Current positioning:** "Play Mallorca's best courses with a PGA pro by your side." + Trip planning.

**Perceived position in market:** Mid-tier golf coach / booking facilitator. Lacks the authority or luxury positioning that commands premium prices.

**The problem:** Your offer is objectively better than competitors (PGA Advanced Professional + 11 years coaching elite in China + Trackman Master), but the site reads as "helpful golf guide" rather than "luxury golf concierge." This costs you €500–1,000 per booking in lower perceived value.

**Evidence:**
- Testimonials are thin (3 people named Jo, Finlay, Adam — no photos, no context)
- No photos of you with clients (biggest trust gap per analytics)
- No story of why you moved to Mallorca or what makes you different from every other golf coach
- Pricing shown (€495–€3,000) but no value narrative to justify premium tier
- No mention of who you've coached or worked with (Pebble Beach, The Open, Evian mentioned only on About page)

## Positioning vs. Competitors

**You vs. GolfEscape** (top booking platform competitor)
- GolfEscape: "Curated golf holidays, local experts, transparent pricing, 30-day guarantee"
- You: "Private days on the courses, trip planning available"
- **Gap:** GolfEscape has 50+ testimonials with photos, case studies, partnership logos. You have 3 unnamed testimonials.
- **Advantage (you):** You're not a middleman; you personally deliver. Should be emphasized.

**You vs. DMC luxury golf operators** (Four Seasons concierge types)
- DMCs: "White-glove service, Michelin dining, champagne on the tee"
- You: "I'll arrange lunch if you want, videographer add-on available"
- **Gap:** You're hedging the luxury positioning ("available"). They're committing to it.
- **Advantage (you):** Actual golf expertise. DMC golf is often arranged through sub-contractors who don't play.

**You vs. Local coaches** (other Mallorca golf pros)
- Most local coaches: Website-free, WhatsApp-only, no reviews
- You: Full website, real brand, established presence in two markets (China + Mallorca)
- **Advantage (massive):** You're more professional, more visible, more credible.

**China Market Positioning (Currently Missing)**
- Your Douyin following is 300M+ views ("Andy 教练")
- Your website has zero Chinese market strategy
- Chinese DMCs (Kuoni, China Golf Holidays) are likely targeting European golf trips but with weak expertise
- **Opportunity:** You're uniquely positioned as a trusted Chinese-speaking golf professional in Europe. This is a moat.

## Missing Positioning Elements

1. **The story** — Why did you leave China? What makes Mallorca the right place to build this? (Currently buried on About page, not front-and-center)
2. **Proof of expertise** — "I've played every course on this island 20+ times. Here's what most golfers don't know..." (Currently: generic course reviews)
3. **Who you've worked with** — Pebble Beach, The Open, Evian, China national team — should appear in hero or homepage. Not hidden on About.
4. **Why the service is worth €3,000** — Signature Day is 1,600 words of features. One paragraph on what the actual day *feels like* for the customer.

## Recommendations

**Reposition from:** "Helpful golf coach who knows Mallorca"
**Reposition to:** "The only PGA Advanced Professional running private coaching days in Mallorca who played at The Open, coached in China, and has turned 50+ golfers into better players"

This is specific, credible, and differentiates you from every competitor on the island.

- **Leak: People looking for course info land here and bounce**

- Form appears on /contact page with no testimonials, trust signals, or "what happens next" message
- **Leak: Unknown friction before submission**
